Scepticism {{ segmented('scepticism', scepticism_modes, prefs.scepticism.value) }}

Scepticism looks for profiles that are too good to be true: a high rating with little corroboration, many overlapping roles, promotional wording, long lists of awards and similar signs. Ratings are capped when a warning is raised. It cannot be switched off.
